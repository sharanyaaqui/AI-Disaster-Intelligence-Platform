Offline Disaster Guides

🌊
Flood

Move to safer or higher ground. Avoid entering moving floodwater.

🌍
Earthquake

Drop, Cover and Hold. Stay away from windows and elevators.

🔥
Fire

Leave using stairs where possible. Stay low if smoke is present.

🌪️
Cyclone

Stay indoors and follow official evacuation instructions.

⚡
Lightning

Move indoors and avoid open areas, isolated trees and water.

🌡️
Heatwave

Drink water regularly and avoid prolonged exposure to extreme heat.
